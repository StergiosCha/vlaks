In English, reluctantly

ΒΛΑΞ (roughly: "dolt"; the word is indeclinable here by decree, in both our languages) is a DJ duo and a party series, four parties, 2016–2019, every year around Christmas, at Fuit Art Cafe, Ηλία Φάσσα 2, Grevena, Greece (2012–2022; the owner now runs Sun Do Each in Thessaloniki, the best sandwiches in town, per the duo, who are objective). Two brothers-in-law-in-aspiration (μπατζανάκια: men married to sisters, except they never found the sisters), one of whom is the idiot and one the broke one. We do not say which is which. A proof assistant was consulted; it did not terminate.

The parties featured Greek 80s-adjacent kitsch played with love and scholarship, teddy bears (dismembered, cherished), lollipops at 2€ per hundred that dye your tongue purple for about a year, a PAOK chant in three renditions, and a dance technique called σκρολτς whose all-time record (411, Düsseldorf, sprained knee) this website's scroll counter respects.

Everything else on this site, the short story, the timeline with its honest red asterisks, the disputes page, the booking terms, is in Greek, because that is the language it happened in. The dialect of Grevena stays untransliterated everywhere. If you have reached this page by accident: προς ΒΛΑΞ. If on purpose: ask a Greek friend, preferably one who has been to a bar in the Balkans after 3am.

Bookings: we do very selective djings. Ask for a quote and we will see if we can do it. No discounts; work is work.

Κολοφώνας: Το όνομα «βλαξ» δεν κλίνεται πουθενά σε αυτόν τον ιστότοπο. Το ελέγχει και μηχανή. Αν βρει ότι κλίνεται, το κόβει: /(ΒΛΑΚ|Βλάκ|βλάκ)/. Οι υποσημειώσεις αριθμούνται συνεχόμενα σε όλο τον ιστότοπο σαν ένα ενιαίο κείμενο, με αρχή το διήγημα. Το πέρασμα του δείκτη πάνω από τη λέξη ΒΛΑΞ προσμετράται στα σκρολτς. Αυτό είναι κανόνας, όχι σφάλμα. Στοιχειοθεσία: Noto Sans Display (φωνή), Commissioner (σκελετός), GFS Didot & Literata (περιθώριο). Καμία καρέκλα δεν χρονολογήθηκε οριστικά κατά την κατασκευή.
